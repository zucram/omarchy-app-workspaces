# Fork attribution

App Workspaces is a fork of [Decent Workspaces](https://github.com/TheTrueFerret/omarchy-decent-workspaces)
by TheTrueFerret, distributed under the MIT license.

The starting point was Decent Workspaces 1.0.0 at commit
`d92284395d7c04d3395f279df998dc19e84d9660`.
The upstream copyright and MIT permission notice are retained in LICENSE.

This fork keeps the workspace-widget foundation and replaces the glyph
presentation with desktop-entry app icons, spatial ordering, scratchpad and
floating-window controls, lifecycle refresh, and a native settings panel.
TheTrueFerret does not maintain or endorse this fork.

Application logos are loaded from the user's installed desktop entries and icon
theme. No third-party logo collection is bundled with the plugin.
